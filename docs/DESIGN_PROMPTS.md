# Design Prompt Library

## Claude Design Version — BLOCK WATCH by HAI

Create a high-fidelity visual concept for **BLOCK WATCH by HAI — The Civic Intelligence Operating System**.

This is not a generic SaaS dashboard. It is the command interface for an intelligent district combining live street analytics, AI vision, digital twins, AR advertising, sponsor verification, community metrics, NPC media hosts, Twitch broadcasting, and Maestro interactive programming.

### Aesthetic

Use a deep midnight navy to deep royal-blue gradient with near-black depth. Surfaces are smoked glass, brushed titanium, graphite, and subtle carbon-fiber texture.

Primary accent: **metallic electric green**, not flat lime and not aggressive neon. The green should resemble anodized automotive/aerospace trim.

Use a signature **specular light sweep / traveling highlight animation** across selected metallic-green edges. The highlight moves slowly like light gliding across polished metal, recurring subtly rather than constantly.

### Shell

- Top command bar
- Left operations navigation
- Large center workspace
- Right context panel
- Collapsible/resizable HAI Command chat
- Bottom system-status strip

### Centerpiece

Show a Puerto Plata/Sosua-inspired district digital twin with live mobility particles, Block Watch Nodes, sponsor zones, AR placements, and flowing aggregate data.

### Modules

Overview, Live Blocks, Digital Twin, Nodes, Trigger Grid, Micro-Ad Lab, REVELV, Community Dashboard, Business Partners, NPC Dispatch, Twitch Control, Maestro Control, NVIDIA Vision, Analytics, Infrastructure, Settings.

### HAI Command

A premium contextual AI copilot, dockable and collapsible. Suggested prompts include Analyze Traffic, Create Micro Ad, Open Digital Twin, Sponsor Performance, Safety Insights, and Revenue Forecast.

### Motion

The interface should breathe: slow graph movement, subtle data particles, restrained reflections, gentle parallax, energy moving through verified pathways, and the signature metallic light sweep. Motion communicates intelligence rather than spectacle.

### Avoid

- Generic Bootstrap/admin dashboard
- Heavy cyberpunk
- Excessive neon
- Surveillance/CCTV aesthetic
- Cluttered sci-fi HUD text
- Game-like UI unless inside a viewer gamification module

### Emotional Target

A city leader should see infrastructure. A business owner should see opportunity. A broadcaster should see a show. A technologist should see an extensible operating system. A resident should see a platform intended to make their block more visible and economically important.

## AR Visual 1 — Central District Neon Heritage

Street-level nighttime view down Puerto Plata's historic central district. Cobblestone street, pastel colonial facades, warm lamps, ocean glow at the vanishing point. Soft AR sponsor panels are spatially anchored to approved buildings. A BLOCK WATCH VERIFIED marker hovers above a participating cafe. Animated digital murals move subtly across second-story surfaces. HAI NPC kiosk in the mid-ground. Warm amber physical light mixes with restrained turquoise/electric-green digital reflections. Caribbean cyber-heritage, photoreal, cinematic, elegant rather than dystopian.

## AR Visual 2 — Block Watch Boulevard View

Slightly elevated balcony POV overlooking a Puerto Plata central-district intersection. Pedestrians and scooters move naturally below. Translucent local-business AR panels attach to facades. A volumetric HAI dog mascot appears from a participating theater facade. Thin aggregate pedestrian-flow lines trace the street. HAI trigger icons appear only at verified activations. Wet cobblestones reflect blue, amber, and metallic-green light.

## AR Visual 3 — Ad Arches

First-person walking perspective through a narrow colonial lane. Iron balconies and historic lamps frame layered but tasteful holographic local ads. One digital gallery promotion, one tropical-drink animation, rotating HAI verification markers, and subtle ground-level mascot animation. Thin data beams connect approved ad zones to a distant HAI node. Preserve architecture as the visual hero.

## AR Visual 4 — Skyline Glow

Elevated descending view down a Puerto Plata street toward the sea. Historic rooftops glow softly from spatial media. Large HAI/REVELV analytics projection on an approved blank wall, subtle active-zone indicators, and BLOCK WATCH STREAM LIVE marker on a lamppost. Golden twilight transitions into cobalt night. Volumetric but restrained future-Caribbean atmosphere.
