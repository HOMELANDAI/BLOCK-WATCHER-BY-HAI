# Commercial Model, Market Prioritization and Civic Dividend

## Why bustling small towns matter
Compact active corridors can have **high signal density** with fewer camera/edge locations than sprawling cities. A single measurement node may support business hours, tourism flow, event impact, safety review and local livestream. This is a capital-efficiency hypothesis to validate, not a guarantee. Quiet towns may be better served by occasional event counts and static reports.

## HAI Micro-Ad Lab offer ladder
**Tier 1 Static Motion:** 5-second logo/motion card; original ideation range $200–$300.
**Tier 2 Dynamic Spot:** 10–15-second creative and social exports; $400–$800.
**Tier 3 Interactive Stream:** 15–30s spot, overlay/poll/QR and reconciled showing; $800–$1,500.
**Tier 4 Signature Campaign:** 3–5 connected creative assets, optional NPC, campaign setup, analytics; $2,000–$5,000.
These are **hypothetical price points from ideation**, not offers/verified unit economics. Before publishing rates model labor, render credits, music/IP rights, editing, translations, media placements, partner fees, revisions, electricity/bandwidth and risk.

## Business case model
For each sponsor/cohort:
- Revenue = creative production fee + media placement fee + verified add-on services.
- Direct costs = creative labor + rendering + assets/licenses + production/approval + streamer/host placement + delivery/reporting + transaction fees.
- Gross margin = revenue - direct costs.
- Contribution margin = gross margin - incremental support/sales/acquisition.
- Retention = paying cohort at period end / paying cohort at start.
- CAC payback = acquisition cost / contribution margin per month (avoid using gross revenue).
- Impressions and sponsor value separately tracked; don't assume physical passerby visibility from virtual AR.

## Revenue experiments
E1: 5 business pilots, some paid at stated prices, some control examples; test willingness-to-pay and repeat intention.
E2: Compare animated creative and static creative on consented digital channels, same audience/period if feasible.
E3: Compare morning vs evening promotional scheduling without over-claiming results.
E4: Test sponsored NPC spot vs conventional creator-read spot on engagement, with disclosure.
E5: Compare tourism trail destination scans with/without digital signage in permitted sites.

## Revenue share and REVELV
REVELV logs *served creative events* with origin, destination, trigger rule, timestamp, render state, expiry, campaign ID and verification. Allocate money only from reconciled invoices/contracts, not impression counter estimates. Separate **ad delivery data** from **commercial payments** from **community funds**.

Potential partnership categories:
- HAI creative & IP production
- Property/site owner rights
- Stream/media producer fees
- Sales referral share
- Dedicated community contribution

Any percentages or local-dividend targets require financial model, agreement, tax assessment and payment controls.

## Civic dividend: ethical local reinvestment
**Vision:** income generated from local participation produces explicit and transparent local benefits. “Ethical trickle-down” should be implemented through contract-defined allocations, not assumed effects.
Policy workflow:
1. Board/owner authorizes policy and eligible revenues.
2. Sponsor contracts specify contribution and financial treatment.
3. Ledger records receivables, reconciled cash, restricted commitments, approvals, disbursements.
4. Local panel proposes projects.
5. Public criteria rank eligible projects: safety, accessibility, tourism benefit, equitable distribution, maintenance, cost.
6. Approving authority signs contract/project work.
7. Publish status: proposed, approved, funded, contracted, completed, inspected.
8. Verify receipts, deliverables and annual independent audit as scale grows.

Avoid promising jobs “created” from spend without payroll/workforce verification; report actual paid creator hours and completed training where documented.

## Client acquisition sequence
Stage A: 3–5 prototype sponsors in Sosúa (restaurants, tour operators, retail, events).
Stage B: interviews and partnered events to test 10-sponsor readiness.
Stage C: Puerto Plata heritage/tourism AR activation with approvals.
Stage D: BID/municipal economic intelligence proposals.
Stage E: Hackensack as cross-cultural, cross-jurisdiction replication test.

## Contractual packages
- Site camera installation and observation agreement.
- Business analytics subscriber terms.
- Sponsor creative statement of work and IP usage/approval.
- Ad inventory, disclosures and frequency terms.
- REVELV delivery definitions and dispute process.
- Municipal data and safety-report use restrictions.
- Privacy, deletion, indemnity, incident response and access retention schedules.
- AR facade/location license with expiry.
- Civic dividend contribution and audit rules.

## Monthly management report
Publish revenue by line of business, direct and acquisition cost, active/renewing sponsors, delivered impressions with verification types, measurement cost/valid node-hour, stakeholder decisions supported, civic fund actual receipts/disbursements, incident escalations and governance outcomes.
