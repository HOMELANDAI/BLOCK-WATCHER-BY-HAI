# BLOCK WATCH by HAI

**The Civic Intelligence Operating System**

BLOCK WATCH by HAI is a HomelandAI civic intelligence, local media, AR advertising, livestreaming, and community-development platform. It turns overlooked streets and intersections into intelligent media spaces where movement becomes data, data becomes storytelling, storytelling becomes revenue, and revenue can be reinvested into local opportunity and infrastructure.

## Core Vision

BLOCK WATCH combines:

- AI street and intersection analytics
- Real-time traffic, pedestrian, bike, and scooter pattern recognition
- Safety and near-miss intelligence
- Hyperlocal Twitch and Maestro.tv broadcasts
- HAI NPC hosts and localized digital personalities
- AR ads attached to buildings and verified physical spaces
- HAI Micro-Ad Lab services for small and mid-tier brands
- REVELV-style ad verification, attribution, and revenue tracking
- Digital twin visualization and smart-district mapping
- Community Vitality metrics and civic reinvestment tracking
- Optional NVIDIA-powered edge AI, computer vision, rendering, and digital-twin infrastructure
- Supabase backend for auth, data, realtime, storage, and edge functions

## Initial Pilot Geography

The first conceptual pilot centers on the Dominican Republic North Coast, especially:

- Pedro Clisante, Sosua
- Historic Central District, Puerto Plata

Expansion candidates include small and mid-size downtown corridors such as Main Street in Hackensack, New Jersey.
